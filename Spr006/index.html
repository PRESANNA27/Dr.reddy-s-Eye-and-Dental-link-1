Prevention & Care

Prevent Problems Before They Start

Expert tips from our specialists to protect your smile and vision every day — because prevention is always better than cure.

Dental Health Prevention

Protect your smile for life

1
Brush Twice Daily — CorrectlyUse fluoride toothpaste and a soft-bristled brush for 2 minutes each session — morning and night. Poor technique is as bad as not brushing at all.
2
Floss Every Night90% of cavities begin between teeth where brushes can't reach. Daily flossing removes plaque and prevents gum disease before it starts.
3
Visit Your Dentist Every 6 MonthsProfessional cleaning removes hardened tartar. Early detection of cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer saves teeth and lives.
4
Limit Sugary & Acidic FoodsSugar feeds bacteria that produce acid, eroding enamel. Rinse with water after meals and wait 30 minutes before brushing after acidic drinks.
5
Never Ignore Tooth Pain or SensitivityPain is your body's alarm. Ignoring dental pain often leads to abscesses, extractions, or costly emergency treatments. Act within 24-48 hours.
6
Guard Your Teeth During SportsUse a mouthguard during contact sports. A single impact can crack or knock out a tooth — prevention costs ₹500, replacement costs ₹15,000+.

Eye Health Prevention

Protect your vision for life

1
Schedule Annual Eye ExamsMany serious eye diseases — glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy — have zero symptoms early on. Annual exams detect problems when treatment is most effective.
2
Control Diabetes & Blood PressureThese are the #1 causes of preventable blindness. Strict sugar and pressure control dramatically reduces risk of diabetic retinopathy and hypertensive eye damage.
3
Wear UV-Protective SunglassesChronic UV exposure causes cataracts and macular degeneration. Always wear CE/UV400-certified sunglasses outdoors, even on cloudy days.
4
Follow the 20-20-20 Screen RuleFor every 20 minutes of screen time, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. This prevents digital eye strain, headaches, and dry eye syndrome.
5
Never Rub Your EyesRubbing can worsen allergies, transfer bacteria, and in corneal weakness cases cause permanent damage (keratoconus). Rinse with clean water instead.
6
Protect Children's Myopia EarlyMyopia (near-sightedness) progresses fastest in children aged 6-14. ZEISS Myovision lenses and outdoor play time can slow progression significantly.
