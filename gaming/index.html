Stay Healthy

How to Prevent Common Problems

Expert-recommended habits that protect your teeth and eyes — prevention is always the best treatment.

🪥
Brush Twice Daily

Use a soft-bristled brush with fluoride toothpaste for 2 full minutes. Reach all surfaces and the gum line. Replace every 3 months.

🧵
Floss Every Day

Flossing removes plaque and food from between teeth where brushes cannot reach — preventing cavities and gum disease at the root.

🥦
Eat Smart

Calcium-rich foods strengthen enamel and bones. Reduce sugar and acidic drinks. Carrots, almonds, and leafy greens promote dental and eye health.

☀️
UV Eye Protection

Wear certified UV-blocking sunglasses outdoors. UV rays accelerate cataracts and macular degeneration. Start protecting eyes early in life.

📱
20-20-20 Screen Rule

Every 20 minutes on a screen, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Reduces digital eye strain and slows myopia progression.

💧
Stay Hydrated

Water flushes oral bacteria and maintains the tear film that keeps eyes moist. Dehydration worsens dry eye and gum inflammation.

🩺
Regular Check-ups

Dental visits every 6 months and eye examinations annually detect problems before they become serious — saving time, money, and discomfort.

🚭
Avoid Tobacco

Tobacco severely damages gums, stains teeth, and triples the risk of cataracts and macular degeneration. Quitting reverses many early effects.
