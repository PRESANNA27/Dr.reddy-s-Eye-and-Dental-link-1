Health Guidance

Prevent Before You Treat

Expert tips to protect your dental and eye health — simple habits that make a lifetime of difference.

01
🪥

Brush Twice Daily

Use fluoride toothpaste and a soft-bristled brush. Brush for at least 2 minutes — morning and night. Replace your brush every 3 months for best results.

02
🧵

Floss Every Day

Flossing removes plaque and food from between teeth where your brush can't reach. This prevents 80% of cavities between teeth and gum disease.

03
🍬

Limit Sugar & Acidic Foods

Sugary and acidic foods erode enamel and feed cavity-causing bacteria. Rinse with water after sugary drinks and wait 30 minutes before brushing.

04
💧

Stay Hydrated

Water washes away bacteria and food particles. Fluoridated water strengthens enamel. Drink at least 8 glasses daily for healthy teeth and gums.

05
🏥

Regular Dental Checkups

Visit your dentist every 6 months even when you have no pain. Early detection of cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer saves treatment cost and pain.

06
🚭

Avoid Tobacco & Smoking

Smoking causes gum disease, tooth loss, and oral cancer. Tobacco stains teeth and significantly increases risk of severe dental and systemic problems.

01
☀️

Wear UV-Protection Sunglasses

UV rays increase the risk of cataracts and macular degeneration. Wear sunglasses that block 99-100% of UVA and UVB rays whenever outdoors.

02
💻

Follow the 20-20-20 Rule

Every 20 minutes of screen time, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. This reduces digital eye strain, dryness, and headaches significantly.

03
🥕

Eat Eye-Healthy Foods

Leafy greens, carrots, fish (Omega-3), and eggs contain lutein, zeaxanthin, and Vitamin A — essential nutrients that protect against macular degeneration.

04
😴

Get Adequate Sleep

Your eyes repair and recover during sleep. 7–9 hours prevents eye strain, red eyes, and blurry vision. Avoid screens 1 hour before bedtime.

05
👁️

Annual Eye Examinations

Many serious eye conditions like glaucoma and retinal disorders show no early symptoms. Annual screening catches them early when treatment is most effective.

06
🚿

Never Touch Eyes Without Washing Hands

Eyes are highly susceptible to infection. Always wash hands thoroughly before touching eyes or handling contact lenses to prevent conjunctivitis and corneal ulcers.

Dental Implants

World-renowned Osstem Implants — Permanent. Natural. Lifetime confidence.

What are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed in the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. We exclusively use world-renowned Osstem Implants — trusted in over 90 countries.

With 30+ years of experience and an international Fellowship in Implantology (Malaysia), Dr. M.V. Sudhakar Reddy ensures exceptional outcomes for every patient.

  • Permanent solution — lasts a lifetime with proper care
  • Looks, feels and functions like natural teeth
  • Prevents bone loss and maintains facial structure
  • No removal needed — fixed in place permanently
  • Osstem implants — globally certified and trusted
1

Initial Consultation & Digital X-Ray

We evaluate your bone density using Digital RVG X-Ray. A personalised treatment plan is created considering your overall oral health.

2

Implant Placement Surgery

Under local anesthesia, the titanium Osstem implant post is precisely placed in the jawbone. The procedure is minimally invasive and virtually pain-free.

3

Osseointegration (Healing Period)

Over 3–6 months, the implant fuses with the bone — a process called osseointegration — creating an incredibly strong, permanent foundation.

4

Abutment & Crown Placement

Once healed, an abutment is attached to the implant. A custom-crafted ceramic crown — matched to your natural teeth color — is permanently fixed.

5

Follow-Up & Lifetime Care

Regular follow-ups ensure long-term success. With proper oral hygiene, Osstem implants can last a lifetime.

BEFORE — Missing Tooth
AFTER — Osstem Implant

Why Osstem Implants?

Osstem is the world's #1 implant system by volume, used in 90+ countries. Their SLA (Sandblasted, Large-grit, Acid-etched) surface technology achieves over 98% osseointegration success rates, ensuring your implant becomes a permanent part of you.

Is dental implant surgery painful? +
The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won't feel pain during surgery. Post-operative discomfort is usually mild and managed with prescribed medication for 2–3 days.
How long do dental implants last? +
With proper oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups, Osstem implants can last a lifetime. The crown portion may need replacement after 15–20 years due to normal wear.
Who is eligible for dental implants? +
Most adults with good overall health and sufficient jawbone density are candidates. We conduct a thorough evaluation including bone assessment to determine eligibility. Conditions like uncontrolled diabetes or active gum disease need to be addressed first.
How many sittings are required? +
Typically 3–4 key visits: initial consultation, implant placement, healing review, and crown placement. The total timeline is 3–6 months to allow proper osseointegration.

Cataract Surgery

Advanced Phacoemulsification — Crystal-Clear Vision Restored

Restoring Clarity with Phacoemulsification

Cataract is the leading cause of blindness worldwide. At Dr. Reddy's Eye Care Centre, we perform state-of-the-art Phacoemulsification — the safest, most advanced cataract removal technique available today.

Dr. G.V. Kamalapriya, trained at Narayana Nethralaya (Bangalore) and Aravind Eye Hospital (Puducherry), brings 23+ years of ophthalmic expertise to every surgery.

  • Phaco laser technology — no-stitch, minimal trauma
  • Same-day procedure — no hospital admission needed
  • Premium IOL lens implantation for crisp vision
  • Vision restoration within days of surgery
  • Experienced surgeon with Narayana Nethralaya fellowship
1

Pre-Operative Eye Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation including biometry for IOL power calculation, corneal topography, and retina check to ensure optimal surgical planning.

2

Phacoemulsification (Laser Removal)

Using ultrasound energy, the cloudy natural lens is broken into tiny fragments and gently suctioned out through a micro-incision (less than 3mm).

3

Intraocular Lens (IOL) Implantation

A foldable premium IOL (intraocular lens) is inserted through the same tiny incision. It unfolds and positions itself perfectly in the eye.

4

Recovery & Follow-Up

Vision improves significantly within 24–48 hours. Eye drops are prescribed for 4 weeks. Full recovery in 4–6 weeks with minimal restrictions on daily activities.

BEFORE — Cloudy, Blurred Vision
AFTER — Crystal Clear Vision

98%+ Success Rate

Phacoemulsification cataract surgery at our centre has an exceptional success rate. Most patients experience significantly improved vision within 24-48 hours and can return to normal activities within a week.

Is cataract surgery painful? +
No. The surgery is performed under topical (eye drop) anesthesia. You may feel mild pressure but no pain. The procedure takes 15–20 minutes per eye.
What is Phacoemulsification vs. traditional surgery? +
Phacoemulsification uses ultrasound energy through a tiny 2-3mm incision — no stitches, faster healing, and less risk compared to the older large-incision technique. Vision recovery is significantly faster.
Can both eyes be operated on the same day? +
Usually, one eye is operated at a time, with the second eye done 1–2 weeks later after confirming good healing of the first eye.
Will I need glasses after surgery? +
With standard IOLs, most patients need reading glasses. With premium multifocal IOLs (available at our centre), many patients can see clearly at all distances without glasses.

Root Canal Treatment

Pain-free endodontics — save your natural tooth

Saving Teeth, Relieving Pain

Root Canal Treatment (RCT) removes infected pulp, eliminates pain, and saves your natural tooth from extraction. Modern techniques make RCT comfortable and efficient.

  • Pain-free procedure with modern anaesthesia
  • Saves your natural tooth permanently
  • Failed RCT management available
  • Calcified root canal specialist treatment
  • Digital RVG X-Ray for precision
1

Digital X-Ray Diagnosis

RVG digital X-ray precisely maps the root anatomy for accurate treatment planning.

2

Pulp Removal

Under anesthesia, infected pulp is carefully removed and root canals are shaped and cleaned.

3

Canal Filling

Canals are filled with biocompatible gutta-percha material and sealed to prevent reinfection.

4

Crown Placement

A custom crown protects the treated tooth and restores full function and aesthetics.

Is RCT very painful? +
Modern RCT is performed under local anesthesia and is essentially pain-free. Most patients report the procedure is no more uncomfortable than having a regular filling.
Can a failed RCT be retreated? +
Yes. We specialize in failed RCT management. Using advanced techniques and instruments, previously treated canals can be retreated successfully in most cases.

When should my child's first dental visit be? +
We recommend the first dental visit within 6 months after the first tooth erupts or by age 1 at the latest. Early visits establish good habits and allow early detection of any concerns.
Are baby teeth really important? +

Periodontics & Gum Care

Comprehensive gum disease treatment and prevention

Strong Gums, Strong Teeth

Gum disease is the leading cause of tooth loss in adults. Our periodontics team provides comprehensive treatment to halt disease progression and restore gum health.

  • Ultrasonic Scaling & Root Planing
  • Soft Tissue Grafting for gum recession
  • Gingivectomy for diseased gum tissue
  • Frenectomy for tongue/lip tie correction
  • Globally recommended sterilization standards
What are signs of gum disease? +
Warning signs include bleeding gums while brushing, swollen or red gums, persistent bad breath, receding gums, loose teeth, or pain while chewing. Early-stage gum disease (gingivitis) is completely reversible with proper treatment.
Is scaling harmful to teeth? +
No — professional scaling removes harmful plaque and tartar that cannot be removed by brushing. It actually protects teeth and gums. Temporary sensitivity after scaling is normal and subsides within a week.

Retina & Diabetic Eye Care

Protecting the window to your world — comprehensive retina services

Advanced Retina Care

The retina is the light-sensitive layer at the back of the eye. Diabetic retinopathy is a leading cause of vision loss in diabetics — early detection is crucial.

  • Diabetic Retinopathy Screening
  • High-resolution Retina Imaging
  • Screening for all Retinal Disorders
  • YAG Laser treatment available
  • Uveal Disorders management
Do diabetics need regular eye checkups? +
Yes — absolutely essential. Diabetic retinopathy has NO symptoms in early stages. All diabetics should have a dilated eye exam at least once a year to detect changes before vision loss occurs.
What is retina imaging? +
Retina imaging uses advanced photography to capture detailed images of the retina. It helps detect diabetic changes, macular degeneration, and other retinal conditions accurately and non-invasively.

Glaucoma Management

The silent thief of sight — detected and treated at our centre

Protecting Your Optic Nerve

Glaucoma is known as the "silent thief of sight" because it causes irreversible vision loss without symptoms until advanced stages. Regular screening is the only way to detect it early.

  • Comprehensive Glaucoma Screening
  • YAG Laser treatment
  • Glaucoma Laser procedures
  • Long-term management and monitoring
  • Neuro-Ophthalmology services
Who is at risk for glaucoma? +
People over 40, those with family history of glaucoma, diabetics, high myopia patients, and people with elevated eye pressure are at higher risk. Annual screening is recommended for all these groups.
Can glaucoma be cured? +
Glaucoma cannot be reversed but can be effectively controlled. With early detection and proper treatment — eye drops, laser, or surgery — vision loss can be prevented or slowed significantly.

Pediatric Eye Care

Protecting young eyes — from newborns to teenagers

Early Eye Care for Lifetime Vision

Children's eyes develop rapidly in the first years of life. Undetected vision problems can permanently affect development. Our comprehensive pediatric eye services ensure your child's eyes are healthy.

  • ROP (Retinopathy of Prematurity) Screening
  • Amblyopia (Lazy Eye) — Orthoptek treatment
  • Preschool & School Vision Screening
  • Squint (Strabismus) Management
  • Myopia Screening & ZEISS Myovision
When should a child's eyes be first checked? +
Newborns should be screened for ROP if premature. First comprehensive eye exam at age 3–4, then before starting school. Children with glasses or lazy eye need more frequent monitoring.
What is Amblyopia (Lazy Eye)? +
Amblyopia occurs when one eye doesn't develop normal vision during childhood. Treatment includes patching the stronger eye, special glasses, or Orthoptek therapy. Early treatment (before age 7) gives the best results.

What is the cornea? +
The cornea is the clear, dome-shaped surface covering the front of the eye. It plays a crucial role in focusing vision, accounting for about 2/3 of the eye's total optical power.
